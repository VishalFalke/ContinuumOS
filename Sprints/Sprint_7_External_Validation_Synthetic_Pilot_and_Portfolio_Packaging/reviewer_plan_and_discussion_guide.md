# Reviewer Plan and Discussion Guide

## Reviewer selection

Target three to five reviewers across these roles: clinician or healthcare professional; care coordination or healthcare operations person; BA, product or implementation professional; EHR/FHIR or healthcare technology professional; and optional RCM or referral operations reviewer.

## Review guide

Cover workflow realism, terminology, ownership, clinical authority, referral handoff, exception handling, AI boundaries, missing failure modes, usability, metrics and unacceptable automation.

Ask what is unrealistic, unsafe, missing or unnecessary; what the reviewer would change; which metric matters most; and what would block adoption.

## Evidence capture rule

Do not call these sessions production user research unless the participants genuinely represent target users and the sessions are documented accordingly. Do not include sensitive personal information in the public case.
