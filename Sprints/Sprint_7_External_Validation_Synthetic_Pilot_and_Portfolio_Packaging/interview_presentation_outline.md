# Interview Presentation Outline

Prepare a concise six-to-eight-slide presentation:

1. Problem and why it matters
2. Current-state breakdown
3. MVP and product boundary
4. Workflow, decision rights and architecture
5. Prototype journey
6. AI controls and failure handling
7. Testing, defects and product changes
8. Validation, measured findings and next decision

The presentation must be deliverable in under ten minutes.
