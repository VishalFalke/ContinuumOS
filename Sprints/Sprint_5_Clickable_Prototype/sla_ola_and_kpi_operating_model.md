# SLA, OLA and KPI Operating Model

## Purpose and boundary

This document operationalises the Sprint 1 measurement definitions without creating new targets or claiming established clinical policy. An SLA measures an outcome/service expectation; an OLA measures the internal handoff needed to support it.

| Control | Start and stop evidence | Proposed accountable role | Escalation / exception rule | Evidence status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Result-to-acknowledgement SLA | Current complete Result Available to current-version human acknowledgement | Clinic physician with Care Coordinator visibility | Overdue work creates visible operational escalation; it never creates acknowledgement | Pilot hypothesis; threshold open |
| Acknowledgement-task OLA | Acknowledgement task assignment to accepted owner action/reassignment | Care Coordinator | Missing or unavailable owner routes to visible escalation | Validation requirement; threshold open |
| Referral-response SLA | Human-approved referral creation to receiving-team response where required | Referral Coordinator | Rejection or missing response remains visible; no automatic rerouting | Pilot hypothesis; threshold open |
| Handoff-package OLA | Human referral direction to human-approved operational package | Referral Coordinator | Missing required evidence blocks package readiness | Pilot hypothesis; threshold open |
| Exception-resolution OLA | Exception creation to verified safe return | Accountable exception resolver | No unsafe state advance while unresolved | Validation requirement; threshold open |

Sprint 1 `success_metrics.md` remains authoritative for KPI calculation definitions, data sources and evidence-status labels. Sprint 6 may use approved illustrative synthetic thresholds for test scenarios; Sprint 7 may report observed synthetic values. Neither action establishes a real operational SLA.
