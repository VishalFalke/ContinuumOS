# Product Roadmap and Delivery Horizons

| Horizon | Intended outcome | Dependencies and decision gate | Evidence status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sprint 5 baseline | Controlled requirements, prioritisation, governance and prototype readiness | Part 3 baseline gate | In progress; documentation only |
| Sprint 6 synthetic prototype | Clickable core workflow, exceptions, audit and controlled AI samples | Approved requirements, test readiness and release gate | Planned |
| Sprint 7 validation | Structured reviewer evidence, synthetic pilot measures and evidence-backed revisions | Release-ready synthetic prototype and reviewer plan | Planned |
| Pilot readiness | Assess participant authority, identity, consent, security, interoperability, AI and operating support | Separate local/participant approvals | Future readiness |
| Later product opportunities | Consider financial workflow detail, preventive health orchestration and real integrations only after discovery | Separate problem, scope and governance decision | Roadmap only |

This is a delivery-horizon view, not a date commitment or deployment plan. The technical roadmap labels in Sprint 4 remain authoritative for represent-only, future-readiness and deferred boundaries.

## Lean delivery model

| Phase | Proposed team focus | Primary dependency | Decision gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Definition and baseline | Product owner, Lead BA, governance/safety reviewer | Canonical workflow, authority and data boundaries | Approve, defer or reject prototype scope |
| Synthetic prototype build | Prototype delivery owner with Product owner and Lead BA control | Approved requirements, fixtures, screen specifications and accessibility controls | Confirm build remains within baseline |
| Synthetic test and release | Prototype delivery owner, Quality/UAT reviewer and Product owner | Executable scenarios, defect triage and AI fallback | Synthetic release decision; no production claim |
| Evidence review | Product owner, Lead BA, planned reviewer inputs | Recorded Sprint 6 evidence and structured review plan | Update roadmap only where evidence supports it |

These are proposed roles and phases for the portfolio case, not staffing, participation, delivery-date or approval claims.
