# Sprint 2 — Care-Episode Operating Model

## Purpose and scope

This artifact operationalises every Sprint 1 core and exception state for Asha Mehta’s synthetic episode, `SYN-PAT-1001`. It defines who owns progress, what must be source-linked, what ContinuumOS records, where AI may assist, when escalation occurs and what automation is prohibited.

The state vocabulary is frozen to Sprint 1. No new state is introduced in this artifact. Cancellation, no-show, test-not-performed, provider rejection, missing-result and stale-task conditions remain operational conditions or reason-coded handling within the existing state model.

## Operating rules applied to every state

- ContinuumOS is an orchestration overlay, not a system of record.
- `Result Available` does not mean clinical review has started, completed or been acknowledged.
- Uncertain patient, encounter or event linkage routes to reconciliation and never silently attaches.
- AI may produce a source-linked episode summary and prepare a source-linked referral handoff after approval. It cannot make clinical, identity, referral or financial decisions. Missing-owner, overdue, duplicate and exception conditions remain deterministic workflow rules; AI-based anomaly detection is deferred.
- Overdue work remains in its current state with deterministic overdue and escalation flags. It is not automatically closed, acknowledged, accepted or confirmed.
- `Episode Completed` means the diagnostic-closure workflow has ended after accountable transfer of the next care step. It does not mean the patient’s broader clinical episode, referral, admission or recovery is complete.

Urgent handling is triggered only by an authorised human decision or a validated source-system flag. ContinuumOS does not infer clinical urgency, and the MVP does not use AI to determine urgency or clinical priority.

## Ownership types

The detailed state tables retain a compact `Responsible role` field for readability. The normalized ownership model below separates the roles that must remain distinct in the later ownership matrix:

| Ownership type | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Clinical decision owner | Person accountable for clinical direction, report review or acknowledgement. | Clinic physician |
| Operational progression owner | Person accountable for workflow progression, ageing, blockers, exception handling and closure evidence. | Care Coordinator |
| Performing role | Person or team executing the operational or source-system action. | Diagnostic operations user |
| Escalation owner | Person who receives unresolved overdue or blocked work at the next escalation level. | Authorised clinical escalation owner or service operations lead |

## Escalation policy for unresolved overdue work

This policy applies across states; it does not create additional states.

| Escalation level | Trigger | Receives work | Required action | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Assigned owner | Task reaches its due threshold or becomes blocked. | Assigned performing role or owner. | Review, update, resolve, reassign or record a blocker. | Due time, reminder, owner response and timestamp. |
| Operational supervisor | Owner misses the due threshold or repeated reminders do not resolve the work. | Care Coordinator’s operational supervisor or diagnostic/referral supervisor. | Confirm ownership, remove operational blocker or escalate further. | Escalation reason, supervisor, action and timestamp. |
| Service manager | Work remains unresolved after supervisor intervention or crosses a defined critical threshold. | Diagnostic, referral, billing or platform service manager. | Coordinate cross-team resolution and confirm a safe next action. | Service-level escalation, decision, owner and resolution evidence. |
| Authorised clinical escalation owner | Clinical review, acknowledgement or follow-up direction is blocked or overdue. | Authorised clinical escalation owner. | Arrange approved clinical coverage or human clinical escalation; no automated clinical decision. | Clinical escalation, assigned clinician, review/decision evidence and timestamp. |

Overdue work remains in its current state with an overdue flag and escalation history. It must not be automatically closed, acknowledged, accepted or confirmed.

## Exception-state operating model

## Result Incomplete reason codes

`Result Incomplete` remains the frozen Sprint 1 exception state, but screens, synthetic test cases and the later UAT pack must preserve the operational cause using a controlled reason code:

| Reason code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `examination_not_performed` | The ordered examination did not occur or was not completed. |
| `report_partial` | A report exists but is incomplete as a document or payload. |
| `required_field_missing` | A required source-linked field or reference is absent. |
| `source_unreadable` | The source content exists but cannot be read or parsed reliably. |

The reason code does not grant clinical meaning or determine urgency. It supports operational routing, auditability and test-case coverage.

| State | Entry trigger | Responsible role | Required data | System action | AI assistance | Exit condition | Escalation condition | Prohibited automated action | Audit evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `Patient Match Failed` | Patient/event cannot be matched under defined deterministic rules. | Care Coordinator / Identity reconciliation reviewer. | Candidate identifiers, source event, conflicting identifiers, match status, reconciliation reason and source references. | Hold the event in the exception queue and prevent episode attachment. | May flag conflicting context; cannot resolve or attach. | Authorised human confirms the match or rejects the event. | Exception ages without reviewer action or source data changes. | Silent attachment, autonomous identity resolution or normal workflow progression. | Match inputs, deterministic result, reason, reviewer, decision, timestamp, correction and return state. |
| `Encounter Missing` | Patient is known but required encounter cannot be confirmed. | Care Coordinator / Identity reconciliation reviewer. | Patient reference, encounter search inputs, source order/event and missing-context reason. | Hold progression and create reconciliation work. | May identify missing encounter context; cannot create or select an encounter. | Human confirms the encounter or records an approved alternative handling. | Reconciliation exceeds policy, source remains unavailable or conflicting encounters exist. | Inventing an encounter, attaching to a likely encounter or bypassing reconciliation. | Search inputs, source responses, reviewer, decision, timestamp, reason and safe return state. |
| `Duplicate Event Suspected` | Repeated order, completion or result event is detected or reported. | Diagnostic operations user / Care Coordinator. | All candidate events, event IDs, timestamps, source, event type and duplicate reason. | Preserve all events, pause downstream progression and create resolution work. | May summarise candidate duplicates; cannot discard or choose the valid event. | Human resolves the duplicate and identifies the event to use. | Duplicate remains unresolved, downstream state would be affected or source conflict persists. | Silent deletion, automatic deduplication or state progression on an unresolved duplicate. | Candidate events, rule/user flag, reviewer decision, retained/discarded status, reason and timestamps. |
| `Result Incomplete` | Examination, report or required source information is incomplete, unreadable or not available as expected. | Diagnostic operations user; Care Coordinator monitors ageing. | Examination status, report payload/status, missing fields, reason code and source references. | Hold acknowledgement progression, display reason and request/retrieve source correction. | May flag missing or conflicting context; cannot judge clinical significance. | Complete current source-linked report/status is verified, or a human records an operational resolution. | Result remains incomplete beyond policy, urgent review need is identified or source is unavailable. | Treating incomplete data as complete, clinically reviewed or acknowledged. | Missing-field reason, source version, request/retrieval events, actor, timestamps and return state. |
| `Amended Result Received` | New report version arrives after result availability or acknowledgement. | Diagnostic operations with clinic physician review. | Prior/current report versions, amendment reason, source event, prior acknowledgement and affected episode state. | Preserve versions, flag amendment and reopen `Clinical Review Pending`. | Summarise version differences only with source references; cannot determine significance or acknowledge. | Current version is reviewed and explicitly acknowledged by a clinician. | Amendment remains unreviewed, material source conflict exists or clinician unavailable. | Carrying forward prior acknowledgement or downstream decision automatically. | Version history, amendment event, reviewer, acknowledgement, timestamps, corrections and state transitions. |
| `Clinician Unavailable` | Assigned clinician cannot act within the applicable operating policy. | Authorised clinical escalation owner / Care Coordinator. | Assigned clinician, task, report version, due time, unavailability reason and reassignment policy. | Flag overdue work, record escalation and support approved reassignment. | May summarise outstanding workflow context; cannot choose a replacement or review clinically. | Replacement clinician is assigned and independently reviews the current report. | No approved replacement, repeated unavailability or urgent escalation requires human management. | Automatic clinical reassignment, acknowledgement or urgency decision. | Unavailability event, escalation history, reassignment, new reviewer and timestamps. |
| `Referral Rejected` | Receiving team rejects the referral or requests a materially different route. | Receiving team for rejection; Referral Coordinator for tracking; clinic physician for any alternative direction. | Referral, destination, rejection reason, receiving actor, source package and current episode state. | Preserve rejection, notify owners and return to a new human follow-up decision. | May summarise rejection context; cannot select a new route. | Responsible clinician records a new approved direction in `Follow-up Decision Required`. | Rejection remains unresolved, patient communication is affected or urgent alternative requires coordination. | Automatic redirection, automatic approval or marking the next step confirmed. | Rejection event, actor, reason, package version, notification, new decision and timestamps. |
| `Payer Information Missing` | Selected financial-readiness work lacks required synthetic administrative information. | Billing/pre-authorisation user. | Missing-field checklist, synthetic payer context, pathway, owner and source references. | Display missing data, assign task and keep `Financial Readiness Pending` visible. | May flag missing package context; cannot infer coverage or fill authoritative data. | Required information is reconciled and readiness work resumes. | Missing data remains overdue, source unavailable or urgent care requires separate clinical handling. | Inventing payer data, approving coverage or blocking urgent clinical direction. | Checklist, source, correction, actor, timestamps, resolution and return state. |
| `Authorisation Denied` | Selected administrative route receives a denial. | Billing/pre-authorisation user; clinic physician/referral roles decide alternatives. | Denial event, route, reason, decision-maker, submitted package and episode context. | Record denial, preserve reason and route to human-controlled alternative decision. | May summarise missing/denied package context; cannot choose the alternative. | Authorised team records a viable alternative in `Follow-up Decision Required`. | No viable alternative, urgent escalation or denial remains unresolved. | Treating denial as care decision, selecting alternative automatically or confirming next step. | Denial event, reason, package, actor, timestamps, alternative decision and audit link. |
| `Integration Unavailable` | Required source system, simulated interface or workflow service is unavailable. | Product/platform administrator with operational owner. | Outage event, affected source/service, episode/state, attempted action and recovery status. | Record outage, preserve pending action, show affected episodes and support approved manual reconciliation. | May summarise impacted workflow context; cannot claim a write or state update succeeded. | Source data and workflow status are verified and safely reconciled. | Outage exceeds policy, affects urgent work or manual reconciliation backlog grows. | Silent retry as success, fabricated source event or automatic safe return without verification. | Outage, attempts, manual actions, source verification, recovery timestamp and state return. |

Recovery from `Integration Unavailable` returns the episode to the **last verified valid state**, not automatically to the intended next state. A restored service must not replay an unverified action or advance the episode twice. Any resumed write or state transition requires verified source data, idempotent event handling where applicable and an auditable human or system reconciliation record.

## No new states in this operating model

No new canonical state is added. The following remain reason-coded operational conditions or use existing states:

- Order cancelled, patient no-show and diagnostic provider rejection remain operational handling around `Diagnostic Scheduled` or `Order Accepted`.
- Test not performed uses `Result Incomplete` with the reason `examination not performed` when a result is expected.
- Result never arrives remains `Diagnostic Completed` with an overdue-result flag; `Integration Unavailable` is used only when a technical outage is evidenced.
- Stale or abandoned work remains in its current state with overdue and escalation history.

Any future state proposal must identify the Sprint 1 gap it addresses, state why existing states are insufficient, define the transition impact and be marked as a proposed change before use.
