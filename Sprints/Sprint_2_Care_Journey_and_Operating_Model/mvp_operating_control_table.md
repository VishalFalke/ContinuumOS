# Sprint 2 — MVP Operating Control Table

## Purpose

This is the operating-control companion to `mvp_diagnostic_workflow.md`. It separates clinical decision ownership, operational workflow ownership and the system actor recording the event. It uses Sprint 1’s canonical state vocabulary.

| State | Entry condition | Clinical decision owner | Operational workflow owner | System actor | Required data | Exit condition | Human gate | Prohibited automation | Audit evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `Order Created` | Diagnostic order is created for the synthetic episode. | Clinic physician | Diagnostic operations / Care Coordinator | Clinic source system and ContinuumOS | Patient, encounter, ServiceRequest, order ID and linkage status | Order is accepted or an exception is raised. | Human resolves uncertain linkage. | Silent attachment or autonomous clinical interpretation. | Order, linkage, actor, timestamp and source reference. |
| `Order Accepted` | Diagnostic provider acceptance event is received. | Clinic physician if clarification is needed | Diagnostic operations user | Diagnostic source and ContinuumOS | Acceptance event, order version and receiving service | Appointment or slot is recorded. | Diagnostic operations accepts or clarifies the order. | ContinuumOS cannot accept on behalf of the provider. | Acceptance event, actor, timestamp and reason. |
| `Diagnostic Scheduled` | Appointment or diagnostic slot is recorded. | Clinic physician for any clinical change | Diagnostic operations user | Diagnostic scheduling source and ContinuumOS | Slot, date/time, status and order reference | Examination is completed, cancelled or not performed. | Diagnostic operations handles rescheduling or no-show. | Inferring completion from a scheduled slot. | Appointment ID, status history and actor. |
| `Diagnostic Completed` | Examination completion is recorded. | Clinic physician if clinical follow-up is needed | Diagnostic operations / Care Coordinator | Diagnostic source and ContinuumOS | Completion event, examination ID and source status | Current report becomes available, or overdue/exception handling begins. | Diagnostic operations confirms completion evidence. | Treating completion as result availability. | Completion event, timestamp, source and missing-field reason. |
| `Result Available` | Current source-linked report status is recorded. | Clinic physician | Diagnostic operations until acknowledgement task is assigned | Diagnostic source and ContinuumOS | DiagnosticReport version, Observations, episode link and availability timestamp | Acknowledgement work is active in `Clinical Review Pending`. | Clinician review remains outstanding. | Treating availability as review or acknowledgement. | Report version, queue entry, owner, SLA and source links. |
| `Clinical Review Pending` | Acknowledgement work is assigned and active. | Clinic physician | Care Coordinator for queue visibility | ContinuumOS; clinician source record | Owner, report version, due time, source report and task | Current report is explicitly acknowledged. | Clinician reviews the current source report. | AI cannot acknowledge or determine clinical significance. | Access event, review event, owner, timestamps and version. |
| `Result Acknowledged` | Clinician explicitly acknowledges the current report version. | Clinic physician | Care Coordinator | Clinician source action and ContinuumOS | Acknowledgement, clinician, role, timestamp and report version | Follow-up decision task is opened. | Explicit human acknowledgement. | Earlier acknowledgement cannot satisfy an amended version. | Acknowledgement event and audit history. |
| `Follow-up Decision Required` | Current report is acknowledged and direction is not recorded. | Clinic physician | Care Coordinator | Clinician source action and ContinuumOS | Decision task, current report, missing context and pathway options | Human selects clinic management, referral or escalation. | Clinician chooses the direction. | AI cannot recommend or select the clinical path. | Decision, actor, reason, timestamp and source references. |
| `Referral Created` | Human approves day-care referral or hospital escalation. | Clinic physician | Referral Coordinator | ContinuumOS and referral workflow | Approved direction, destination, handoff task and prerequisites | Receiving team accepts, rejects or remains pending. | Human approval before task creation and handoff use. | AI cannot create or send a referral autonomously. | Approval, task, package version and destination. |
| `Referral Accepted` | Receiving team accepts the required handoff. | Clinic physician for clinical direction | Receiving team | Receiving workflow and ContinuumOS; Referral Coordinator tracks/records the response | Acceptance, destination, appointment/facility status and prerequisites | `Next Step Confirmed`, or selected planned path enters `Financial Readiness Pending`. Urgent approved hospital escalation may use the controlled `Next Step Confirmed` branch while financial readiness remains visible and unresolved. | Receiving team acceptance. | Automatic acceptance or redirection. | Acceptance/rejection, actor, reason and timestamp. |
| `Financial Readiness Pending` | Selected planned path requires administrative readiness after clinical and referral prerequisites. | Clinic physician remains owner of clinical direction | Billing/pre-authorisation user prepares, submits and tracks; Care Coordinator verifies workflow closure | Administrative source and ContinuumOS | Payer/admin checklist, owner, status, submission and decision | Readiness is recorded, or `Payer Information Missing` / `Authorisation Denied` occurs. | Authorised financial decision-maker records the final decision; urgent clinical direction is not blocked. | Autonomous authorisation or coverage decision. | Checklist, decision, actor, timestamp and reason. |
| `Next Step Confirmed` | Human-confirmed direction has owner, destination/team, timeframe, task, communication and required acceptance. | Clinic physician for direction | Care Coordinator | ContinuumOS with human confirmation | Closure evidence, patient communication, acceptance and financial status where applicable | Diagnostic-closure workflow is transferred and enters `Episode Completed`. | Care Coordinator verifies closure conditions. | Confirming without evidence or implying broader care completion. | Confirmation event, owner, destination, timeframe and communication. |
| `Episode Completed` | MVP diagnostic-closure workflow has ended after accountable transfer. | Original clinical owner for later clinical work | Care Coordinator | ContinuumOS | Completion event, closure evidence, unresolved-work check and audit links | Terminal state for this MVP workflow; broader care may continue elsewhere. | Human closure verification. | Closing the broader patient care episode or rewriting history. | Completion event, actor, timestamp and preserved audit trail. |

## Ownership interpretation

- **Clinical decision owner** decides clinical direction and performs clinical review or acknowledgement.
- **Operational workflow owner** manages progression, ownership, ageing, blockers, exceptions and closure evidence.
- **System actor** is the system or user action that records the event; recording an event does not transfer decision authority to the system.
