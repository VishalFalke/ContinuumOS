# Sprint 1, Part 5 — Care-Episode State Model

## Status

Working draft for Sprint 1 review. This model defines proposed workflow states for the synthetic diagnostic-to-referral case. It is not a claim about a production implementation or a universal healthcare workflow.

## Purpose and operating boundary

The state model gives the Care Coordinator and supporting teams one shared view of where Asha Mehta's care episode is in the diagnostic-closure workflow, who owns the next action and whether progress is blocked.

It begins when a diagnostic order is created and ends only after a human-confirmed next care step is recorded and the episode can be closed. It does not make clinical, patient-matching, referral or financial-authorisation decisions autonomously.

> Result Available does not mean Clinically Reviewed. A result requires explicit human acknowledgement before the episode can progress to a follow-up decision.

SLA and ageing expectations below are proposed operating-policy fields. The seven-day case will demonstrate that they can be made visible; it will not claim a universal time limit or achieved performance.

## Core care-episode states

| State | Entry condition | Accountable owner | System action | AI assistance | Human decision or control | SLA / ageing expectation | Exit condition and typical blockers |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Order Created** | A diagnostic order for the linked patient and encounter is received or recorded. | Clinic physician / diagnostic operations | Creates or updates the episode; records source order and linkage status. | May summarise available episode context only. | A human resolves any uncertain patient or encounter linkage. | Await acceptance within locally defined operational policy. | Exits when the diagnostic provider accepts the order. Blockers: uncertain linkage, incomplete order context, unavailable integration. |
| **Order Accepted** | The diagnostic provider accepts the order. | Diagnostic operations user | Records acceptance and makes scheduling work visible. | None required. | A human resolves order clarification where needed. | Await scheduling within locally defined operational policy. | Exits when an appointment or diagnostic slot is scheduled. Blockers: missing order details, capacity issue, integration unavailable. |
| **Diagnostic Scheduled** | A diagnostic appointment or slot is recorded. | Diagnostic operations user | Displays scheduled status and relevant event details. | None required. | A human manages rescheduling or cancellation. | Age from scheduled date/time until completion or a documented change. | Exits when the diagnostic event is completed. Blockers: cancellation, no-show, unavailable diagnostic capacity. |
| **Diagnostic Completed** | The ultrasound has been performed and completion is recorded. | Diagnostic operations user | Records completion and awaits the result event. | May identify incomplete source context for later handoff, without judging clinical significance. | A human addresses a missing or incomplete examination record. | Await result availability within locally defined diagnostic policy. | Exits when a complete result is available. Blockers: result incomplete, integration unavailable. |
| **Result Available** | A source-linked diagnostic report is available for the confirmed episode. | Diagnostic operations user until acknowledgement work is assigned | Preserves report version; creates the acknowledgement work item; assigns owner and SLA. | Produces a source-linked episode summary and may flag missing clinical context or report-version concerns. | No clinical inference is made; the responsible clinician must review the source report. | Acknowledgement ageing starts at result availability. | Exits to **Clinical Review Pending** when review work is active. Blockers: result incomplete, amended report, clinician unavailable, uncertain linkage. |
| **Clinical Review Pending** | A complete result awaits clinician review and acknowledgement. | Clinic physician | Shows assigned clinician, SLA age and escalation rules; records review activity. | May surface source-linked context and operational-attention recommendations. | Clinician reviews the source report; this cannot be completed by AI or an automated rule. | Age until explicit acknowledgement; missed SLA is a rule-based workflow condition. | Exits when the clinician explicitly acknowledges the result. Blockers: clinician unavailable, result incomplete, amended result received. |
| **Result Acknowledged** | The clinician has explicitly acknowledged the current report version. | Clinic physician | Records user, role, timestamp and report version; opens the follow-up-decision task. | None required for acknowledgement. | Clinician acknowledgement is required. Acknowledgement of an earlier version does not cover an amended report. | Follow-up-decision ageing starts after acknowledgement. | Exits to **Follow-up Decision Required**. Blockers: none inherent; an amended result reopens review. |
| **Follow-up Decision Required** | The current report version is acknowledged and a care direction is not yet recorded. | Clinic physician | Creates a decision task, displays available pathway options and records the decision audit event. | May prepare a source-linked summary and identify missing context; does not recommend a clinical direction. | Clinician chooses clinic management, referral or hospital escalation. | Age until a human-approved direction is recorded. | Exits when one approved care direction is recorded. Blockers: clinician unavailable, missing information requiring review. |
| **Referral Created** | A clinician approves referral or escalation and a referral task is created. | Referral Coordinator | Routes the task, records referral destination and tracks prerequisites, owner and SLA. | After approval, may draft a source-linked referral handoff package for human review. | Referral or escalation approval is human-controlled; AI output cannot create or send a referral. | Age until receiving-team response where that pathway requires acceptance. | Exits when the receiving team accepts the referral, or routes to an exception on rejection. Blockers: missing documents, receiving-team capacity, referral rejected. |
| **Referral Accepted** | The receiving team accepts the referral or escalation handoff. | Receiving team for acceptance; Referral Coordinator for tracking and downstream coordination | Records acceptance, receiving-team details and subsequent prerequisite status. | May help prepare a source-linked, human-reviewed handoff package. | Receiving-team acceptance is required only for referral or escalation pathways. | Age until the pathway's remaining requirements are complete. | Exits to **Financial Readiness Pending** only on selected planned paths, or to **Next Step Confirmed** when no financial-readiness work is required. Urgent approved escalation may confirm the next step while financial readiness remains open. Blockers: appointment/facility not confirmed where required, missing prerequisite information. |
| **Financial Readiness Pending** | A selected referral or hospital-escalation path requires administrative readiness after clinical and referral prerequisites are met. | Care Coordinator for workflow visibility; Billing/pre-authorisation user for preparation and tracking | Shows readiness status, owner, dependency and ageing; records submitted or returned items. | May flag missing package context; does not make a coverage or authorisation decision. | The Authorised financial decision-maker records final financial authorisation. | Age against the defined administrative readiness policy for the selected path. | Exits when readiness work reaches the documented state required for the next step, or routes to an exception. Blockers: payer information missing, authorisation denied, missing prerequisites. |
| **Next Step Confirmed** | A human-approved care direction has a named owner, destination or responsible team, documented timeframe, and recorded patient communication; receiving-team acceptance and appointment/facility confirmation are present where required. | Care Coordinator | Validates and records the closure criteria; captures communication status and audit events. | May draft a patient message or handoff package, both subject to human approval before use. | A human confirms that the pathway-specific conditions are satisfied and approves patient-facing clinical content where needed. | Monitor until the documented next step is handed off or clinic follow-up responsibility is clear. | Exits when the episode has reached its defined workflow completion condition. Blockers: missing owner, destination, timeframe, patient communication, required acceptance or appointment confirmation. |
| **Episode Completed** | The confirmed next step has been handed off or clinic management responsibility is clear, and required audit evidence is recorded. | Care Coordinator | Locks the workflow completion event while preserving audit history and source links. | None required. | A human remains accountable for correcting any later discovered workflow error; a new or amended result can create renewed work rather than silently altering history. | No active ageing after completion; retain audit record according to the proposed case policy. | Terminal state for this MVP episode. Blockers: incomplete closure evidence or unresolved exception. |

## Exception states

Exception states are explicit work states, not silent data corrections. They remain visible in the exception queue with an accountable owner, source evidence, age, resolution action and audit history. Resolution returns the episode only to the appropriate safe core state; it never bypasses a required human decision.

| Exception state | Trigger | Accountable owner | Required handling and safe return point |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Patient Match Failed** | The event cannot be linked to the synthetic patient under defined matching rules. | Identity reconciliation reviewer | Do not attach the event. Reconcile manually; return to the relevant source-event state only after human confirmation. |
| **Encounter Missing** | A patient is known but the required encounter cannot be confirmed or retrieved. | Identity reconciliation reviewer | Hold workflow progression and reconcile the encounter. Return to the relevant source-event state after human confirmation. |
| **Duplicate Event Suspected** | A repeated order, completion or result event is detected by deterministic checks or identified by a user. | Diagnostic operations user / Care Coordinator | Preserve both source events, investigate and mark the valid event through human review. Return to the appropriate current state; do not silently discard history. |
| **Result Incomplete** | The report or required source information is missing, partial or cannot be read reliably. | Diagnostic operations user | Request or retrieve the complete result. Do not present the episode as ready for clinical acknowledgement; return to **Result Available** only when a complete source-linked report is available. |
| **Amended Result Received** | A new report version is received after an earlier result was available or acknowledged. | Clinic physician, with diagnostic operations support | Preserve all report versions and earlier acknowledgement history. Reopen **Clinical Review Pending** for the amended version; a new human acknowledgement is required. |
| **Clinician Unavailable** | The assigned clinician cannot review or decide within the applicable operating policy. | Authorised clinical escalation owner | Assign approved clinical coverage and coordinate the escalation. The replacement clinician must independently review and acknowledge the source report. |
| **Referral Rejected** | The receiving team declines the referral or requests a materially different route. | Receiving team for rejection; Referral Coordinator for tracking; clinic physician for any alternative direction | Record the rejection reason and notify the responsible clinician. Return to **Follow-up Decision Required** for a human-approved alternative; do not automatically redirect. |
| **Payer Information Missing** | Selected financial-readiness work lacks required synthetic payer or administrative information. | Billing/pre-authorisation user | Request or reconcile the needed information. Return to **Financial Readiness Pending** when complete; no autonomous coverage determination occurs. |
| **Authorisation Denied** | A selected escalation path receives a denial or cannot proceed under the proposed administrative route. | Authorised financial decision-maker for the administrative decision; clinic physician for any alternative clinical direction | Record the decision and reason. Route to a human-controlled alternative care or administrative decision; do not mark the next step confirmed solely because a request was submitted. |
| **Integration Unavailable** | A source system, simulated interface or required workflow data is temporarily unavailable. | Product/platform administrator with operational owner | Record the outage and affected episode. Use an approved manual reconciliation process; return to the relevant state after source data is verified. |

## Non-negotiable controls carried into all states

- Uncertain patient, encounter or event linkage always routes to reconciliation. It must never silently attach to an episode.
- Clinical report review, acknowledgement, follow-up direction, referral or escalation approval, receiving-team acceptance, patient-facing clinical message approval and final financial authorisation remain human-controlled.
- Deterministic rules manage missing owner, missing acknowledgement, expired SLA and duplicate-event indicators. These conditions must not be presented as AI detection.
- AI outputs must be source-linked, reviewable and logged with approval, correction, rejection or override history. They cannot independently change the care direction or workflow closure state.
- An amended result invalidates automatic reliance on an earlier acknowledgement and requires renewed human clinical review.

## Human-controlled transitions

The following transitions cannot be completed by a deterministic rule or AI output. The system can prepare work, enforce required evidence and record the decision, but a named authorised person must take the action.

| Transition | Required human control | System and AI boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Any event to a linked episode | The Identity reconciliation reviewer confirms a patient, encounter or event match where the defined rules are uncertain. | The system may apply matching rules and route exceptions; it cannot silently attach uncertain information. AI may flag conflicting context only. |
| Clinical Review Pending → Result Acknowledged | The clinic physician reviews the current source report and explicitly acknowledges it. | The system records the action and version. AI may summarise sources but cannot acknowledge a result. |
| Follow-up Decision Required → clinic management, referral or escalation path | The clinic physician records the clinical follow-up direction. | The system creates downstream tasks only after this decision. AI may identify missing context but cannot choose a direction. |
| Referral Created → Referral Accepted | The receiving team accepts the referral or escalation handoff where that pathway requires acceptance. | The receiving team is accountable for acceptance or rejection; the Referral Coordinator tracks the response and coordinates downstream work. AI may prepare a draft handoff package, but it cannot send, accept or approve a referral. |
| Referral Accepted → Financial Readiness Pending or Next Step Confirmed | The Care Coordinator verifies pathway requirements; financial-readiness work begins only where the selected planned path needs it. For urgent, human-approved hospital escalation, the next step may be confirmed while financial readiness remains open. | The system applies visibility and dependency rules; it cannot decide that financial authorisation is approved. |
| Financial Readiness Pending → Next Step Confirmed | The Authorised financial decision-maker records any final financial-authorisation decision required by the selected planned path; the Care Coordinator verifies closure evidence. | The system records readiness and decision status. AI may flag missing package context only. |
| Any patient-facing clinical message or AI handoff draft → operational use | The Referral Coordinator approves the operational handoff package; the Clinic physician approves patient-facing clinical content where clinically contextual. | The system blocks unapproved use and records the responsible reviewer, timestamp and outcome. |
| Amended Result Received → Clinical Review Pending | A clinic physician re-reviews the amended source report and gives a new acknowledgement when appropriate. | The system preserves versions and reopens review. Neither an earlier acknowledgement nor AI output can satisfy the new review requirement. |

## Allowed MVP pathway behaviour

The product supports three human-confirmed end paths. Each path uses the same diagnostic closure controls until **Follow-up Decision Required**; they diverge only after a clinician records the direction.

### 1. Clinic-management path

The clinician selects clinic management. The Care Coordinator records the named clinic owner, the follow-up timeframe and approved patient communication. No receiving-team acceptance is required. Once these closure conditions are met, the episode moves to **Next Step Confirmed**, then **Episode Completed**.

### 2. Day-care referral path

The clinician approves a day-care referral. The system creates **Referral Created** and the Referral Coordinator prepares the handoff. The receiving team must accept the referral. If the selected route has no financial-readiness dependency, confirmed acceptance, named destination, documented timeframe and approved patient communication allow the episode to move to **Next Step Confirmed**. If administrative readiness is required, it follows the selected-path rule below before confirmation.

### 3. Hospital-escalation path

The clinician approves hospital escalation. The system creates **Referral Created**, and the receiving team must accept the handoff. For selected escalation routes, **Financial Readiness Pending** starts only after the clinical direction and referral prerequisites are present. Financial readiness is supporting work, not the primary completion condition and not an autonomous approval. The next step is confirmed only after the pathway's required acceptance, financial status, named owner/destination, timeframe and patient communication have been recorded.

Surgery, discharge and home recovery are outside this MVP. A referral rejection, missing prerequisite, financial denial or unavailable integration routes to its explicit exception state rather than being treated as completed.
